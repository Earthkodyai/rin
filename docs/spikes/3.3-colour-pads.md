# Task 3.3 measurement: can a just-woken user clear the colour pads within 2 minutes?

**Device:** Xiaomi 14T, Android 15 / HyperOS 2.0 · **Build:** debug, VRoid sample model (Rin's own model is not in yet) · **Game:** `mission/PadsGame.kt` (rules), `alarm/ring/PadsBoard.kt` (screen)
**Why:** D17 (ADR 0001) makes games with Rin the way an alarm stops. Colour pads is the first. It must not become the thing that keeps a real user from stopping their alarm, and it must not be so easy that it proves nothing. Plan phase-3: dev 5 rings to tune, freeze, then held-out 10 from bed (≥ 3 in the dark), pass within 2 min in ≥ 9/10.

## The game as built (before any data)
- 2×2 pads, red / blue / yellow / green, each with its own note (C5 E5 G5 C6 on the alarm stream). Taps count on touch-down.
- The ring screen shows Rin full body and a **Let's play** button; the game starts only on that tap, so no demo plays to someone still asleep. (Design call in 3.3: the plan did not say when the first demo starts.)
- Rin's hand (rendered from the model, from above, entering from the top edge; the user's pick) glides onto each pad and presses it. No pad twice in a row, so every step is a visible move.
- 3 rounds, lengths 3 → 4 → 5. After the hand leaves, **3 s** for the first tap and for each next tap (D17), shown as a draining bar.
- Wrong or slow: the pads drop away, Rin sulks (pouty mood + huff) with one of three lines for that kind of miss (the user's pick, set A, never the same line twice in a row), for `scoldMs`, then the **same round** with a new sequence.
- Tone: "Let's play" and each right tap count as progress (quiet at 0.15 for 30 s); misses and demos do not, so an idle player gets the full tone back.
- Log: `MISSION_PASSED type=pads … game=colour_pads rounds=3/3 mistakes=m timeouts=t rightTaps=r seed=s tookMs=…`.

## Protocol (frozen before any data)
1. **A ring.** I schedule a debug alarm from the PC, `DebugAlarmReceiver --es cmd add --ei sec <45–120, I pick it> --es mission pads`. The tester lies down in bed with the phone where it normally sleeps, screen off, and does nothing until it rings. They then stop it the way they would on a real morning. Snooze and the emergency hold are allowed, and both count as a fail.
2. **Timing** comes from the ring log (`ring_events` rows): from `RING_START` to `MISSION_PASSED` of the same ring. Mistakes, timeouts and `tookMs` come from the `MISSION_PASSED` detail.
3. **Dev: 5 rings** with the room as it is. After each, the tester answers one question in chat: Rin's demo was *too fast / fine / too slow*. The starting timings are `moveMs 450`, `pressMs 450`, `exitMs 400`, `scoldMs 2500` (a 3-step demo takes 3.1 s, a 5-step one 4.9 s).
4. **Tuning rule (pre-registered).** If **3 or more** of the 5 answers are *too fast*, `moveMs` and `pressMs` each grow by 75 ms. If 3 or more are *too slow*, each shrinks by 50 ms. Otherwise they stay. `tapTimeoutMs` (3 s) and the round lengths are D17's and are not tuned. The resulting `PadsRules` and this file are committed **before** the first held-out ring.
5. **Held-out: 10 rings**, at least **3 with the lights off** (as dark as the room is at wake-up), not all in one sitting if the tester prefers. Scored once, with the frozen rules.
6. **Pass bar:** **≥ 9 of 10** held-out rings end in `MISSION_PASSED` within **120 s** of `RING_START`.
7. **Reported, not scored:** the demo answers, mistakes and timeouts per ring, time from ring to "Let's play", whether the notes were heard and the hand could be seen in the dark.
8. **Slips.** If the tester reports a slip before any data is opened (started the game before lying down, woke to someone else's noise…), that ring is excluded unopened and replaced, and the deviation is written below first.
9. **No pass:** the numbers and the reason go here. Before any change, the options go to the tester (e.g. 4 s per tap, shorter rounds, a replay of the demo); nothing is retuned silently on held-out data.

**Known limit (not measured here):** a scheduled ring in the evening is not a real wake-up. The 14 real mornings of task 3.7 are the true test; this measurement only makes sure the game is fair to a sleepy player.

**Tuning (rule 4):** 5 of 5 *fine*, so the timings stay: `moveMs 450`, `pressMs 450`, `exitMs 400`, `scoldMs 2500`, with the answer window opening as her last press ends (bug fix below). dev-4 and dev-5 (fixed build) logged `earlyTaps=0`. The tester was unsure whether dev-5's one miss was their own. **Frozen for held-out** with this commit; from here each miss is logged with the pad tapped, the pad due and the wait in ms (`misses=`), which changes no rule.

## Deviations
- **Board look changed before dev-1** (the tester's call): the dark panel behind the pads was removed (pads on the screen's own colour, unlit pads a solid darker shade), and the hand fades in over the top fifth of the board. Timings unchanged.
- **dev-1 fired with the screen on** (the protocol asks for it off). It is kept, as a dev ring only tunes the demo speed. It showed HyperOS keeping the ring's full-screen heads-up over the top pads for the whole ring; from dev-2 the notification is reposted on a quiet channel while the ring page is open (`ring_screen`), and the heads-up is gone.
- **Hand fade after dev-3** (the tester's report from dev-2: on the lower pads the arm's cut showed as a hard line): the hand image's own top 45% now fades in too. Visual only, timings unchanged; dev-4 on.
- **Smoke ring, 2026-09-29 10:18 (not data).** Before this protocol was committed, a first build rang once to check the game ran end to end: passed in 32 s, 1 mistake, 0 timeouts. The hand then drew at about half a pad wide, too small to follow, and was enlarged to 0.9 of a pad before any dev ring. That ring is not counted anywhere.
- **Taps as her hand left were dropped (bug, fixed after dev-3).** The tester reported right taps judged wrong. The game opened the answer window only after her hand's 400 ms exit and silently ignored taps before it, so a quick first tap was lost and the next right tap was judged against the first pad. Now the window opens as her last press ends (the exit is added to the first tap's 3 s), and taps during the demo are counted (`earlyTaps` in the log). The mistakes in dev-1 to dev-3 may partly be this bug.
