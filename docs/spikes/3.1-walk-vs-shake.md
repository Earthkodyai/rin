# Task 3.1 measurement: can the walk mission tell walking from shaking?

**Date:** 2026-09-28 · **Device:** Xiaomi 14T, Android 15 / HyperOS 2.0 · **Harness:** `app/src/debug/.../debug/StepLabActivity.kt` (debug builds only), scoring in `tools/steplab/`
**Why:** on the first device run of the walk mission, the tester passed 30/30 steps by shaking the phone in place. Both Android step sensors (counter and detector) counted the shakes. The mission is meant as friction, not anti-cheat (plan 05c), but a shake from bed is almost no friction.

## Protocol (frozen before any data)
1. **Blind 20 s trials.** `adb shell am start -n io.github.earthkodyai.rinalarm/.debug.StepLabActivity --es trial <label>`, then press Start. The app records step-detector and step-counter events plus the accelerometer and gyroscope (~50 Hz) to an app-private CSV. It never shows a count. Motion only; no camera, no microphone, no location. CSVs stay on the PC, git-ignored.
2. **Conditions.**
   - *walk*: hold the phone in front of you as if watching the ring screen, and walk normally around the house.
   - *shake*: sit or lie on the bed and shake the phone however you like to make steps count.
   - *swing* (held-out only, reported, not scored): sit on the bed and swing the phone up and down in a walking rhythm, the deliberate cheat.
3. **Dev:** 3 walk + 3 shake. Features are computed per trial (`tools/steplab/analyze.py`). Candidate features: step rhythm (median interval, coefficient of variation), accelerometer magnitude (p95, max), gyroscope magnitude (p95), and how much of the motion is vertical (share of linear acceleration along gravity).
4. **Rule pick on dev (pre-registered).** The rule works per step, because the mission counts step by step: a step-detector event counts when the motion in the 2 s before it meets the rule. Step-counter events cannot be judged one by one, so a filtered mission counts detector steps only (the data also shows how many steps the detector misses against the counter). Among single-feature thresholds and pairs, take the rule that **keeps ≥ 90% of the steps in every dev walk** and removes the most dev shake steps. A tie goes to the simpler rule, then to the widest margin from the nearest walk. The rule and its thresholds are committed before held-out.
5. **Held-out:** 3 walk + 3 shake + 2 swing, scored once with the frozen rule.
6. **Pass bar:** every held-out walk keeps **≥ 80%** of its steps (30 counted steps then take at most ~25% longer; a real walk must never be blocked, since the alarm would then stop only through the emergency hold), **and** at least 2 of 3 held-out shakes keep **≤ 20%** (30 counted steps would need five times the shaking). Swing is reported honestly, whatever it shows.
7. **No pass:** the mission stays as it is (friction only), the limit is written down, and the QR sticker (3.2) carries the "out of bed" job.
