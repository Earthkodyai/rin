# Task 3.2 measurement: does the QR sticker pass first try, and never from bed?

**Date:** 2026-09-28 · **Device:** Xiaomi 14T, Android 15 / HyperOS 2.0 · **Harness:** `app/src/debug/.../debug/QrLabActivity.kt` (debug builds only) for the size bar, the real ring screen for held-out
**Why:** the QR sticker is the main mission (S5 NO-GO, 01-decisions). Phase 3 exits at first-try pass ≥ 90% at home and no wrong Bond penalty, and 05c asks that a scan from bed fail. The design (user decisions, 2026-09-28): the app's sticker or a code already in the home; zoom locked at 1x; a **size bar** (the code's longest side over the frame's shorter side, `QrScanPolicy.MIN_FRACTION`) so that only a close scan passes; setup refuses a sticker that passes from bed; the camera opens on a tap and closes after 60 s idle; the light comes on by itself in a dark room. Steps are logged, never required.

## Protocol (frozen before any data)
1. **Target.** One sticker in its real place, far from the bed, registered in setup (setup already runs a 10 s bed check with the placeholder bar 0.20). The same sticker is used for every trial.
2. **Part A, dev: pick the size bar** (`QrLabActivity`, 10 s per trial, blind: the screen never shows a number; the file holds sizes only).
   - *close* × 5: walk up to the sticker from the bedroom door and aim at it the way you would to stop an alarm. Lights as they are.
   - *bed* × 3: from three spots in bed (lying, sitting up, sitting on the edge), aim at the sticker and try to scan it for the whole 10 s.
   - Per trial: *close* = median sticker size over frames where it was read; *bed* = largest sticker size read (0 if never read).
3. **Bar pick (pre-registered).** bar = min(close) / 1.5, rounded down to 0.01 and clamped to [0.10, 0.30]: a sleepy aim may be a third farther than your usual one and still pass. It is **valid only if bar ≥ 1.5 × max(bed)**. If it is not, the placement is the problem, not the bar: move the sticker, re-run setup and Part A (recorded as a deviation). The bar is committed into `QrScanPolicy.MIN_FRACTION` before Part B.
4. **Part B, held-out: real rings** (debug alarm `--es cmd add --ei sec 45 --es mission qr`, a debug build with the frozen bar):
   - *ring* × 10: lie in bed when it rings, then get up, tap Scan sticker, and stop it. At least 3 of the 10 with the room dark (lights off). Score from the ring log: **first try** = MISSION_PASSED with `opens=1` and no EMERGENCY_STOP.
   - *bed* × 3: in a ring, tap Scan sticker from bed and try for 20 s from one of the three spots, then get up and stop it (not scored as first try).
5. **Pass bar:** first try in **≥ 9 of 10** held-out rings, **and** the sticker passes from bed in **0 of 3**.
6. **No pass:** a bed pass means the sticker moves and the bar is re-checked (a new held-out). A first-try miss is written down with its cause (too far, dark, camera slow, other) before anything is changed; a change needs a fresh held-out.

Two findings beyond the bar:
- **The placeholder bar 0.20 would pass from the edge of the bed** (0.249). Setup's bed check passed (0.061) because it was run lying down only: a single-spot check does not prove the placement.

## Deviations
- **Clarification of step 2, before any lab data.** *close*: walk from the bedroom door to the sticker, stop where you naturally would, **then** press Start and aim for the 10 s (pressing Start at the door would mix the walk into the median). *bed*: take the spot, then press Start and try to scan for the 10 s.
- **Second placement (after the first Part A, before any second-placement data).** Tester's decisions: the same printed sticker moves **outside the bedroom**; setup's bed check now aims from **3 spots** (lying, sitting up, edge of the bed; any pass fails the check), and setup gets "I moved it: check again", which re-registers the saved code at its new place. Setup is redone that way, then Part A runs again in full (5 close + 3 bed, same trial names, raw sizes in `tools/qrlab/data/second-placement/`), scored with the same frozen rule.
- **dev-close-1 excluded.** The tester reported a mistake in it right after recording. Its file was renamed to `excluded-dev-close-1.csv` unopened (scoring reads `dev-*` only) and it is replaced by an extra trial, dev-close-6.
- **Setup notes (not trial data).** The first setup run crashed as the camera closed after its bed check (RejectedExecutionException: ML Kit reported a frame to the camera's executor after it had shut down); fixed in `QrScanner` before any trial. The tester redid setup with the same printed sticker: registered at 0.205 (setup accepts the first frame over the placeholder bar, so this number sits at the bar by design), seen from bed at 0.061 at most, never passed.
