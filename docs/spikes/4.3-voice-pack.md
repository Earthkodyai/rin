**Started:** 2026-09-30 · **Tools:** [`tools/voice/pack.mjs`](../../tools/voice/pack.mjs) (generate → trim/loudness → mouth tracks), [`pack-qa.mjs`](../../tools/voice/pack-qa.mjs) (automatic checks), [`qa.html`](../../tools/voice/qa.html) (listening page, launch entry `rin-voice-qa`) · takes, request log and built clips stay in `tools/voice/pack/` (git-ignored)

## 1. Model: eleven_v3, not the new v4 (before buying)
ElevenLabs released **Eleven v4** on 2026-09-28 ([blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/eleven-v4)), two days before this task, and its docs now call v3 "previous generation". v4 carries no alpha/beta label and is said to follow audio tags more closely, so it was a fair candidate.

- **Method:** the 10 S4 audition lines ([`spikes/s4-voice/lines.json`](../../spikes/s4-voice/lines.json), same tags) in Rin soft on v4 (free plan, comparison only), against the S4 v3 clips. Blind A/B per line, sides shuffled ([`spikes/s4-voice/ab.html`](../../spikes/s4-voice/ab.html)).
- **Result:** the user preferred **v3 on 9 of 10 lines** (v4 won L03). They flagged crackle on L06 and some other clips.
- **Credits on the free plan:** v3 about 0.4 credit per character (15 for 38), v4 about 0.11 (77 for 706). Probably launch pricing; re-checked on the paid plan below.
- The user also heard an S4 line asking them to find something green (L05). That set dates from the photo mission, which was dropped in D16/D17; the real script has no such line (checked).

## 2. Crackle: what the ear heard, measured
No clip clips outright (no sample ≥ 0.98), but the v3 clips run hot (peaks −0.4 to −1.3 dBFS), and three have sudden jumps between adjacent samples (> 0.5 full scale): L02 (4.61 s, and 5.41 s just before the end), **L06 (3.45 s, the line the user flagged)**, L10 (2.67 s). The v4 clips have none. The user chose automatic handling:
- Every take is checked before listening (`pack-qa.mjs`): `crackle` (any such jump), `clipped`, `pace` (words per second outside 1.0–4.5), `gap` (a stall over 1 s), `empty`. A flagged take is retaken up to twice, and the take with the fewest flags is kept.
- The build normalizes to −16 LUFS integrated with a −2 dBTP true-peak target (the hot peaks come down), adds 10 ms fades at both ends (end clicks) and trims silence to 80 ms before and 150 ms after. Every built MP3 is re-measured, and the bar is −16 ± 1 LU with a true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
- **Dry run** on the 30 free-plan Repeat clips (not for release): all 30 at −16.4 LUFS with a true peak of −2.8 dBTP or lower, and loudnorm stayed linear on every clip. The checks flagged 6 of the 30 (crackle R06, R07, R17, R22; pace R12; gap R25), so retakes should cost about 20% extra. Whether those flags match the ear gets scored on the listening page (the FLAGGED line in the summary).
