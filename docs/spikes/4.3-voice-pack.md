# 4.3: Rin's voice pack (the paid ElevenLabs month)

**Started:** 2026-09-30 · **Tools:** [`tools/voice/pack.mjs`](../../tools/voice/pack.mjs) (generate → trim/loudness → mouth tracks), [`pack-qa.mjs`](../../tools/voice/pack-qa.mjs) (automatic checks), [`qa.html`](../../tools/voice/qa.html) (listening page, launch entry `rin-voice-qa`) · takes, request log and built clips stay in `tools/voice/pack/` (git-ignored)

## 1. Model: eleven_v3, not the new v4 (before buying)
ElevenLabs released **Eleven v4** on 2026-09-28 ([blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/eleven-v4)), two days before this task, and its docs now call v3 "previous generation". v4 carries no alpha/beta label and is said to follow audio tags more closely, so it was a fair candidate.

- **Method:** the 10 S4 audition lines ([`spikes/s4-voice/lines.json`](../../spikes/s4-voice/lines.json), same tags) in Rin soft on v4 (free plan, comparison only), against the S4 v3 clips. Blind A/B per line, sides shuffled ([`spikes/s4-voice/ab.html`](../../spikes/s4-voice/ab.html)).
- **Result:** the user preferred **v3 on 9 of 10 lines** (v4 won L03). They flagged crackle on L06 and some other clips.
- **Credits on the free plan:** v3 about 0.4 credit per character (15 for 38), v4 about 0.11 (77 for 706). Probably launch pricing; re-checked on the paid plan below.
- The user also heard an S4 line asking them to find something green (L05). That set dates from the photo mission, which was dropped in D16/D17; the real script has no such line (checked).

## 2. Crackle: what the ear heard, measured
No clip clips outright (no sample ≥ 0.98), but the v3 clips run hot (peaks −0.4 to −1.3 dBFS), and three have sudden jumps between adjacent samples (> 0.5 full scale): L02 (4.61 s, and 5.41 s just before the end), **L06 (3.45 s, the line the user flagged)**, L10 (2.67 s). The v4 clips have none. The user chose automatic handling:
- Every take is checked before listening (`pack-qa.mjs`): `crackle` (any such jump), `clipped`, `pace` (words per second outside 1.0–4.5), `gap` (a stall over 1 s), `empty`. A flagged take is retaken up to twice, and the take with the fewest flags is kept.
- The build normalizes to −16 LUFS integrated with a −2 dBTP true-peak target (the hot peaks come down), adds 10 ms fades at both ends (end clicks) and trims silence to 80 ms before and 150 ms after. Every built MP3 is re-measured, and the bar is −16 ± 1 LU with a true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
- **Dry run** on the 30 free-plan Repeat clips (not for release): all 30 at −16.4 LUFS with a true peak of −2.8 dBTP or lower, and loudnorm stayed linear on every clip. The checks flagged 6 of the 30 (crackle R06, R07, R17, R22; pace R12; gap R25), so retakes should cost about 20% extra. Whether those flags match the ear gets scored on the listening page (the FLAGGED line in the summary).

## 4. Vowel mouth shapes on Rin's voice (carried over from 2.4)
In 2.4 one set of vowel centroids failed across Kokoro voices (22/60 held-out). The app only ever plays Rin's prerecorded voice, so 4.3 calibrates on her alone.
- **Frozen first:** [`eval/vowels-rin.json`](../../tools/voice/eval/vowels-rin.json) was committed (cbe5c2e) before any clip: dev = the 2.4 dev words, held-out = 20 words never used before, 4 per shape. Each word is read alone ("hot."), with no tag, in Rin soft on eleven_v3 (77 credits).
- **Dev:** 9/20 with the old centroids, then `eval.mjs fit` on the dev medians: 14/20 on the same words.
- **Held-out, run once:** **18/20 (90%)** against a 60% bar (chance 20%). The misses: *she* read as u, *new* read as e. Every a, e and o word was right. With 20 words the 95% interval is roughly 68–99%, so the pass holds at its low end.
- **Result:** `pack.mjs` writes mouth tracks with shapes (`SHAPES_ON`), and `centroids.json` is now Rin's. Other voices (the free-plan debug clips) keep loudness only. The eye check on the phone is part of 4.4.

## 5. Listening QA, round 1: 66 of 191 to redo
The first full pack (MP3 source, automatic retakes) was built at −16.6 to −15.9 LUFS, true peak ≤ −2.6 dBTP. The user listened to all 191 clips and sent back **66** (125 passed). Their notes: crackle (23, often on one stressed word such as *watch*, *job*, *cup*, *time*), hiss (10), muffled (12), the two sentences of a line in different tones (5), and 20 of the 30 Repeat sentences monotone (all read `[warmly]`). A few were specific: *the pillow* mispronounced, emergency.02 too sarcastic, no pause after *Hi*, odd *hey* and *yes*.
- **The automatic checks did not match the ear.** The crackle check caught 1 of the 23 crackling clips, and 2 of the 3 clips it flagged for crackle passed. Of 16 flagged for a long gap, 11 were sent back, but mostly for other reasons. From round 2 on the user's ear is the bar, and each clip gets 3 candidate takes to pick from.
- **Is it our processing?** A blind test on 8 of the returned lines, 5 versions each (`exp-render.mjs`, `exp.html`, 351 credits):

| Version | normal | crackle | hiss | muffled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw take from ElevenLabs (MP3 128k) | 2 | 4 | 1 | 1 |
| same take after our build | 2 | 4 | 1 | 1 |
| new take, MP3 source, stability 1 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 0 |
| new take, PCM 24 kHz, stability 1 | **5** | 2 | 0 | 1 |
| new take, PCM 24 kHz, stability 0.5 | **5** | 2 | 1 | 1 |

  Raw and built got identical marks on every line, so the artifacts come from the TTS output, not from our processing. PCM was cleaner than the MP3 source, and stability made no difference. won.hard.04 crackled in all five versions. One listener and 8 lines, so this points the way rather than proving it. The formats Starter offers are MP3 44.1 kHz 128k or PCM up to 24 kHz (PCM 44.1 kHz needs Pro, MP3 192k needs Creator).
- **Round 2 changes (the user's picks):** source `pcm_24000`, stability 0.5, 3 candidate takes per returned clip with the pick made by ear on the QA page. Repeat sentences get one tag each by meaning ([`repeat-tags.json`](../../tools/voice/repeat-tags.json)). Text fixes: emergency.02 → "Okay, it's off now. Please take care of yourself." `[concerned]`, app.evening.03 → "Hi… Let's get tomorrow ready.", tap.head.02 gets `[gently]`.

## 7. Round 4: lines cut off at the end
The user heard some lines "cut before she finished" and asked for a check across every clip.
- **Measured on all 191 chosen takes:** 69 raw takes end mid-sound: the last 40 ms sits 17–35 dB under the peak, against a median of −45 for a clean end. So the TTS output itself stops early. Separately, our −45 dBFS silence trim clipped the soft end of the last word on quiet PCM takes (back.last.01: 2.80 → 2.72 s). The trim now uses −60 dBFS.
- **What stops eleven_v3 cutting** (`exp3-tail.mjs`, 365 credits): the plain text cut 5 of 6 lines again. A trailing " …" and a trailing " [short pause]" each ended all 6 cleanly (−42 to −86 dB). Blind by ear, the user picked `[short pause]` (best on 5 of 6, nothing spoken aloud, 2 lines trailed off in both endings). Every request now ends with it, and the build trims the pause away.
- **New check `cut`** (last 40 ms louder than −35 dB under the peak). Automatic retakes now fire only on measurable faults (`cut`, `clipped`, `empty`), and crackle and gaps are left to the ear. `pack.mjs recheck` found 69 cut + 2 clipped, and `regen` remade the 71 (89 takes, 1,938 credits). Afterwards **0/191** chosen takes and 0/191 built clips end mid-sound. The user passed 68 of the 71 and picked from 3 takes for the other 3.

## 8. Air ("wind") at word ends: a known limit
The user heard air on almost every clip and asked for the standard fix. Two blind tests (`exp4-air.mjs`, `exp5-air.mjs`, no credits, 6 lines each):

| Clean-up | air gone | side effect |
|---|---|---|
| gate (downward expander), FFT denoise (`afftdn`) | 1/6 | – |
| de-esser, strong (i 0.7, m 0.7), alone or with the others | **6/6** | wobble on 3/6, muffled 1/6 |
| gentle de-esser, high-shelf −6 dB @ 6.5 kHz, −4 dB @ 8 kHz, gentle mix | 1/6 | – |

The air sits in the hiss band during speech, not in the gaps, so only a de-esser strong enough to pump touches it. One line wobbled in every version, even through a static EQ that cannot pump, so that wobble was in the take itself (it was retaken). The user had said the air was acceptable if it could not be fixed cleanly, so **no clean-up filter is applied** (`POLISH = ''`). Clean-up filters, when used, run after the gain so their thresholds sit at −16 LUFS on every clip. DeepFilterNet (a 1 GB AI denoiser) was not tried: it is built to keep speech, and this air is part of the speech.

## 9. On the phone (Xiaomi 14T)
Every line and sentence played from `voice/rin/` (`clip=true`), with its mouth track (lip start 70–150 ms after the audio). The user's reports and the fixes (commits 0196cd4, 9926720):
- **No mouth on the win screen:** the joy and clap gestures held VRoid's `happy` (wave: `smile`) at full weight over her line. A gesture's face now scales by 1 − 0.75 × talk, where talk eases over 0.12 s. Fixed, and checked by the user.
- **The game went on while she scolded:** her scolds (2.6–7 s) outlast the games' 2.5 s pause. The next round now waits for her line (`RinMouth.awaitQuiet`, max 10 s) and is timed from that tick, so demo speed, swaps and tap time match the held-out (amends D23). Checked by the user with a wrong cup.
- **Mouth off on the opening line:** it started ~1 s into the ring while her page took ~1.5–1.9 s to load. It now waits for her face (max 5 s) plus 0.3 s. She greets by mood as her face appears (wave, or a yawn when sleepy) and the line skips its own gesture, because two waves 0.3 s apart made her hand jerk. The user picked a greeting every time.
- **Subtitles too big and over her:** they now sit in a band under her (16 sp; two lines reserved on the ring screen, under the strip on home only while she speaks).
- **A shake at the end:** at a pass the card and controls vanished and her view grew ~250 dp in one frame. They now stay as invisible space for every game, as the cups already did.
- **A white screen before she speaks:** the window used the light theme on a dark-mode phone. Night themes now match the dark screen (#1A1C20 on the first frame, measured).
- Lines repeat within a day by design (D22: daily by date, no history). Two test rings on one day hear the same opening, intro and closing lines.
