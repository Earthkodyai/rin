## Findings during the run
1. **Rin's eyes missed the user at the clap (S1, the tester: "not looking straight at me, slightly off").** Measured in the desktop preview in the ring screen's full-body framing, VRoid sample: her eyes pointed **6° above the camera** in the cheerful mood and **12.7° above** in the proud mood that the pass shows. Causes: the full-body camera sits at her waist height, so meeting the user means looking down 11°; her head turned only a third of the way; VRoid's eyes turn only a ninth of what is asked (10° for 90°); and the proud mood raises her chin 5.7°. The strip never showed it because its camera is at her head height. **Fix (behaviour.ts `aimCamera`):** toward the user the head turns the whole way and cancels the mood's own pitch, and it turns against the upper body's sway; anything else (saccades, glances, a finger, the cups) still gets a third of the way. At her cup table the old behaviour stays (she watches her hands). **After:** within ±1.8° on both axes in the proud mood (the head's own idle sway), within ±1.5° in the strip; cup table unchanged (the formula reduces to the old one there). On the phone (extra pads ring, 02:24) the tester saw her look straight at them. Commit 3670429.
2. **The pads hand's thumb (S6 and S7, the tester).** First it curled under the palm like a hook; opened flat (first fix), it read longer than the index, which points down at the pad and so shows shorter from above. **Fix (main.ts `rinHand`):** the thumb stays open but angles down toward the pad like the index. Tuned by rendering variants of the hand image in the desktop preview; the tester approved it on the phone (extra pads ring, 02:49). Commit 062b426.
3. **2D cups blended into the table (S9, the tester).** The red cups and the wood were almost equally bright (contrast 1.07:1), apart only by hue. **Fix (CupsBoard.kt):** a dark walnut table with a lighter wood edge, ≥ 3:1 (WCAG's non-text bar), held by `CupsBoardColoursTest`. The tester approved it on the phone (extra crashed cups ring, 02:58). Commit 12d1265.
