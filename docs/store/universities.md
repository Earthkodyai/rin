# Tournament universities (G.7)

The tournament's university list and logos: where they come from, where the files live, and how a university gets its name or logo taken down. Code: `app/.../tournament/Universities.kt` (ids, short names, English and Thai names, rank); the server accepts exactly these ids (`firebase/firestore.rules`, `validUniversity`; OnlineLimitsTest keeps the two in step).

## The list

- **Ranking:** Ranking Web of Universities (Webometrics, CSIC Madrid), **July 2026** edition, the **top 50 in Thailand** (the user's pick, 2026-10-03). Campuses count as their university (none of the 50 is a campus of another).
- **Where it was read:** webometrics.info did not resolve from this PC on 2026-10-03 (and webometrics.org is a known look-alike, not the ranking), so the table came from the copy on a Thai university site: https://webometrics.pcru.ac.th/article/92 (Phetchabun Rajabhat University, "Webometrics Ranking กรกฎาคม 2026"), parsed from its HTML.
- **Caveat:** SUT's news post on the same edition (https://www.sut.ac.th/en/news/detail/1/news20260710e) gives slightly different numbers (Chula world 397 vs 337 here; places 7–11 in another order), maybe a corrected release. Who is in the top 50 is the same in both for every place SUT lists.
- **Rejected:** QS Asia 2026 lists only 43 Thai universities (no NIDA, STOU or Assumption); THE Asia 2026 lists 31.
- **Order in the app:** the picker is A–Z by English name with the Thai name under it (the user's pick); `rank` is kept for the doc and tests only.
- **Changing the list** (a new edition, a takedown): edit `Universities.kt`, the list in `firestore.rules` (same order), deploy the rules (`npx firebase deploy --only firestore --project <id>`, firebase/README.md), and keep or remove the logo file. Rows already posted with a removed id show the id in capitals and can't be updated until the player picks again.

## Logos

- **Files:** `<id>.png`, 128 px square with a transparent margin, in `D:/RinAlarm-unis` on the developer's PC; `rin.unis` in local.properties points there, and the build copies them to `assets/unis/` (`copyRinUnis`). They are **never in the repo**. A build without them (CI, clones) shows each university's short name on a colour, and `bundleRelease` refuses a Play bundle without them.
- **Source:** 41 from each university's infobox on Thai or English Wikipedia (the user approved downloading from Wikimedia, 2026-10-03), picked by `D:/RinAlarm-unis/review/logos2.py` (squarest seal or emblem, not wordmarks or photos). The 9 Wikipedia lacks came from each university's own website (the user: "try to find the other 9 first"), except RMUTK, whose site is behind a bot wall: its mark is the letters row of the public-domain RMUTK wordmark on Commons. Candidates and the crops are in `D:/RinAlarm-unis/review/cands/`; the contact sheet the user approved is `review/contact.png`. SSRU's only emblem online is 64×80 px, so it is upscaled and soft.
- **Rights:** the marks belong to the universities. A Wikipedia "public domain" or "fair use" tag is about copyright on Wikipedia, not permission to use a trademark in an app, and players accepting the rules gives no rights either (told to the user on 2026-10-02, phase-games.md; the user accepted the risk). Mitigations: the files stay out of the public repo, the app says "not affiliated with or endorsed by" in the rules, the picker and the leaderboard, and any university can ask for removal.
