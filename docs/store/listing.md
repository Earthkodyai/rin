**Full description** (4000):

```
Rin is your morning buddy. When your alarm rings, she is there on screen to wake you up, and the alarm stops when you beat her at a quick game.

THREE WAKE-UP GAMES
• Colour pads: watch Rin's hand, then tap the colours in the same order.
• Cup shuffle: follow the ball as she shuffles three cups, then find it.
• Repeat after Rin: say a short English sentence back to her (or tap the words).
Pick a game per alarm, or let Rin pick a different one each day.

A CHARACTER, NOT A BEEP
• Rin is a 3D anime character who reacts while you play: cheerful, sleepy, or a little pouty when you snooze.
• She speaks English with her own voice, with subtitles.
• Four alarm themes (magic, café, arcade, forest) or a classic beep.
• Day and night looks: a candy morning by day, a starry sky at night.

MADE FOR REAL MORNINGS
• Rest day and sick day: one tap, and your next alarm has no game (and a sick day rings softer).
• Snooze when you need it, and a 3-second hold to stop the alarm in an emergency.
• When you start, Rin walks you through your first alarm, with a practice round of its game. Try any game again from the alarm editor.
• Diagnostics checks that your phone lets alarms ring on time, with fixes for phones that block them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline: the app has no internet permission.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases.
• The microphone is used only during Repeat after Rin, on your phone, and nothing is recorded.

About Rin: she is a fictional character. Her lines were written in advance with the help of AI and her voice was generated with AI; nothing is generated while you use the app.

The app is in English. Meant for adults (18+).
```
